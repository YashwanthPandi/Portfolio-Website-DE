## Privacy Policy

This policy is intended to be consistent with applicable United States privacy laws, including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), and other applicable state and federal privacy requirements.

By using this website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

This notice is provided in a general format and is intended to inform visitors about our privacy practices in a clear and transparent manner.

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that identifies, relates to, describes, or is reasonably capable of being associated with you or your device. The categories of personal information we may collect include:

- Identifiers, such as your IP address, browser information, device identifiers, and approximate location.

- Internet or electronic activity information, such as pages visited, time spent on the website, referring pages, click behavior, and interaction data.
- Professional or employment information, if you choose to provide it in connection with inquiries or communications.
- Cookies and similar tracking data used for analytics, site performance, and user experience optimization.

We collect this information directly from you, automatically when you visit the website, and from third-party analytics providers.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the website.
- To respond to your inquiries, comments, or requests.
- To analyze website traffic and user behavior to understand engagement and improve content and functionality.
- To monitor site performance and detect technical issues or misuse.
- To comply with legal obligations and protect the rights, safety, and security of the website and its visitors.

We do not sell personal information in the ordinary course of business.

## 3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

This website uses cookies, pixels, beacons, and similar technologies to improve the user experience, remember preferences, measure traffic, and understand how visitors interact with the website.

These may include:

You may manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of some parts of the website.
